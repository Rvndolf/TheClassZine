About Me

Hello! My name is Assel Abache. I am a first-year BSCS student at Bicol University, from Cagbacong Legazpi City, Albay. I chose BSCS because I have a passion for technology and programming. I hope to learn more about software development and improve my coding skills.

🕒 A typical day for me

My everyday life mostly revolves around mundane tasks such as doing house chores, feeding my pets, finishing all the pending schoolwork, and commuting. During school days, especially now at college, I spend my free time learning new skills such as programming, communication, and time management.

My Path Into Computing

📖 Read my story

How I became interested in computing progressed gradually in my early years. Initially, I hardly saw myself pursuing computer science, since I used to easily get overwhelmed by the fast-paced advancement of technology. But my high school experience helped me discover my passion for computing. I took Computer Systems Servicing (CSS) as my TLE specialisation during Junior high, and as I learned, I slowly grew fond of computers and configuring them as I saw the complexity of technology I once took for granted. From that, it became my stepping stone towards being enthusiastic about computer software. In the present, I don't regret choosing this path because, as I learn and discover new things in this field, I see myself grow academically and personally.

Reflection

💭 Read my reflection

I learned a lot during the creation of my personal webpage. I learned that creating a webpage isn't just about writing, designing, and turning it into functional content; it also teaches us what the environment of a browser actually is. Every day, we are exposed to different web creations—from simple scrolling to productive tools. Yet it is easy to overlook the creativity and logic behind this because, after all, we are after its functionality and features, rather than the semantic tags and elements they cleverly used. But revising my previous About Me page taught me to appreciate web development more, as well as the importance of expressing oneself in every way possible.
