Reflection

While making this webpage, I learned that you really can make it as long as you do your best. Looking at this latest webpage about myself, I can see that it improved a lot compared to my earlier About Me activity—even though it still has some flaws, the progress is there. I learned how to organize content with semantic sections, tables, and lists, which gave this page a much cleaner structure and layout than my first activity.

Building this page felt just like a journey where you do not always know where you are going or how far you still have to go. There are challenges along the way, and I know it gets hard—sometimes giving up even feels like an option. However, I realized that you can make it if you just keep trying, and taking the time to know yourself more makes everything possible.
