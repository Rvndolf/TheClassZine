DOSSIER // 02 ORIGIN TRACE // WHY CS

Why I Chose Computer Science

curiosity.log RUNNING

It Started With A Screen

Ever since I was a child, I have been curious about technology — especially computers. I remember watching videos on an old PC with a CRT monitor. Back then, it was so much fun just to sit in front of that glowing screen and explore.

Then my uncle gave me a more modern PC. I watched movies on it all day, and I also started playing around with it — opening folders, changing settings, and figuring out what the device was capable of. I kept exploring and exploring, and the more I discovered, the more I wanted to know.

“Yes!! I finally found a way to satisfy my curiosity even further.”

8TH GRADE // CHOOSING COMPUTER PROGRAMMING

In 8th grade, we were given the chance to choose a course for our 9th and 10th grade path. I didn't think twice. I chose Computer Programming, and that decision became years of learning. Today I can build apps, create chatbots, and develop AI tools that help with modern problems.
