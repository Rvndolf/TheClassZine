About Me

Hello! My name is Mikee Llona. I am a first-year BSCS student at Bicol University. I am from the town of Daraga in Albay province. I chose BSCS because I have a strong interest in technology and programming, and I hope to learn valuable skills that will help me pursue a career in the field of computer science. My Path into Computing

My interest in computing started with curiosity about how the apps, games, and websites I use every day actually work. In senior high school, I took the STEM strand, which gave me a strong foundation in science and mathematics and helped me realize that I enjoy solving problems.

That curiosity led me to choose Computer Science at Bicol University. Every class so far has shown me how much there is to learn, and it has made me more excited about where this field can take me.

Reflection

Building this page helped me understand how HTML gives a web page its structure, from headings and lists to tables, images, and links. It was a good first step in turning an idea into something I can actually see in the browser.

Working with CSS showed me how much design matters. With CSS, I could control the colors, fonts, spacing, and layout, and make the page look clean and organized instead of plain. I also learned how to use hover effects, a responsive layout that adjusts to different screen sizes. It took some trial and error to get everything working, but fixing those problems taught me to be patient and to look carefully at my code.

As I continue my journey in college, I hope to gain more experience, improve my abilities, and prepare myself for future opportunities in the field of technology.
