My Path Into Computing

Since I was little, technology has always been one of my greatest interests. I would often open broken phones just to see how their components worked. As I grew older, I became even more curious about how games and technology were created. This curiosity eventually led me to become fascinated with coding and the logic behind it.

Additionally, this interest aligns with my way of thinking, as I often enjoy finding solutions and fixing problems whenever I can. Because of this passion for problem-solving and technology, I chose to pursue a degree in Computer Science.
