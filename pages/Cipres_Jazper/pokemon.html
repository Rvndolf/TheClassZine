Completed Pokémon ROM Hack Games & Ratings

Here are my personal thoughts and ratings on completed ROM hacks I've played:

Source: PokeHarbor

Pokémon Unbound — 10/10

It's just peak. The custom CFRU engine, modern quality-of-life features, and deep exploration make it an absolute masterpiece of a hack.

Source: PokeHarbor

Pokémon Radical Red — 9/10

It's hard and I had a hard time fighting the Elite Four, but it was peak. The strategic depth and strict level caps forced me to truly master team building.

Source: YouTube Gameplay

Pokémon Red Chapters — 10/10

It follows the story of the manga and the events were peak enough to make me cry. The narrative execution is unmatched.

Source: Pokemon Quetzal Official

Pokémon Quetzal — 8/10

Customizable with cool features like following Pokémon and multiplayer elements, but ultimately too easy for me.

Source: Pokemon Elite Redux Official

Pokémon Elite Redux — 9/10

The introduction of innates and multi-abilities is so insane and lets you build completely overpowered and creative Pokémon teams.
