My Reflection

What have I learned?

After learning about HTML and CSS and applying my knowledge to the activity, I found that HTML structures and fundamentals are very coherent. It is straightforward and allows you to construct your webpage with semantic, well-organized code. CSS was where I faced initial challenges, learning how selectors and how certain properties worked in transforming an element's properties.

On a personal level, I learned that I genuinely enjoy the creative problem-solving side of coding. I found myself having that same curiosity I had when I was a kid, trying to be as optimistic as I can with every problem that I encounter with regards to what I'm intending to build.
