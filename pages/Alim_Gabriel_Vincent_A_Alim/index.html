My path into computing

I chose Computer Science because I found that creating something by my own hands feels incredibly satisfying and fulfilling, my goal is eventually making a game of my own that I can be proud of. Unfortunately tragedy struck, Due to conflicting priorities I was unable to refine my coding and had to put it aside for the meantime while I focused on more pressing priorities throughout my senior highschool. Now that I am in college, I am determined to learn everything that I can and make my past self proud!
