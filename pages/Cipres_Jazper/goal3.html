Objective Overview

To me, programming and songwriting are not just technical exercises or pastimes, they are medium through which we connect with people on a human level. My deepest dream is to combine my computer science background, my love for music, and my passion for youth leadership into projects that heal, inspire, and uplift lives.

"Whether it's a song that articulates the silent pain someone couldn't express, or code written to solve a real community problem, true achievement lies in making someone's world a little brighter."

The Impact & Purpose Roadmap

Using music as a vehicle for emotional healing and self-expression, writing stories that make listeners feel understood.

  • [✓] Composed original tracks (such as "Di Pa Rin" and "Clinging") that turn personal heartbreak and vulnerability into shared comfort.

Building a life's work where technology and music come together to leave a lasting positive mark on the world.
