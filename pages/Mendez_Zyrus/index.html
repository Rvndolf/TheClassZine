Reflection

What was easy?

The HTML part and the initial CSS with all the fonts and coloring. Having the pleasure of doing it myself with suggestions of course and it actually working. Drafting the overall structure of the code. Of course, the initial booting up of the website gave me a sense of accomplishment and the drive to keep on making it.

What was confusing?

Well, almost every part of the process. The initialization was enjoyable. However, the part where I need to specify certain id or class names and relating it in the css part. I had to manually adjust the layout on different screen sizes and ensuring automatic resizes based on the device. Most of all is the flex layouts which moves around with every change which sometimes does not even work as expected.

What is one thing I would improve?

Everything from making the basic structure instinctively down to honing and adapting more of the specifiers. As I've seen some of my classmates have done, I would like to improve on the overall layout and design of the page. I know it's not a competition... well mostly, I am still a beginner and am amazed at the things I did with some suggestions of course.
